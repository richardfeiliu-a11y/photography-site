## What These Sites Have in Common

Facts:
- Plain "under construction" page with no archive, no gallery, no contact.
- No evidence the maintainer plans to migrate existing content when the new site is live.
- Older work effectively invisible until the new site launches (if ever).

## Anti-Pattern Lesson

Facts: A maintained site should never degrade to a "this site is being rebuilt" placeholder. Two patterns avoid this:

1. Keep the old site live at a /legacy/ path while building the new one.
2. Maintain a static list of past work even when individual galleries are broken (at least URLs/names + external links to where the work lives).

Interpretation: For a long-term personal archive, "absence during upgrade" is the single biggest threat to discoverability. Plan a non-degrading transition state from day one.
