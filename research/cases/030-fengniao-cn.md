## Potential Lesson for Our Project (interpretation)

Like Tuchong (case 029), confirms that the Chinese photography web is dominated by community platforms, not personal archives. A personal-archive site in the style we are designing would be unusual in the CN context - which may be a feature (differentiation) or a liability (no established conventions to inherit).
