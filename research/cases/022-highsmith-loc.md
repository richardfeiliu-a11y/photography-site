## What Works

Facts: place hierarchy + rich per-item metadata is the proven model for multi-thousand-photo place-based archives.

## What Does Not Work

Facts: institutional scale brings navigation weight; mass-archive aesthetic dilutes per-photo impact.

## Potential Lesson for Our Project (interpretation)

If our archive ever approaches 500+ galleries, a place-first hierarchy with rich metadata is the only validated path. The trade-off is per-photo intimacy vs archive discoverability.
