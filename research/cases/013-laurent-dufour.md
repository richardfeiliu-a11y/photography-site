## What Works

Facts: clear thematic naming; one city focus; collective membership adds discoverability; news page keeps site alive.

Interpretation: thematic-within-city works when the photographer'\''s practice is one-place-focused.

## What Does Not Work

Facts: 9 series titles already feel like a long sidebar; news page mixes updates with photo posts.

## Potential Lesson for Our Project (interpretation)

If our practice were single-city, a thematically-titled series structure would be coherent. Our brief asks for multi-place, so the lesson is: title specificity matters - "Paris Late Night 2024" carries more information than "Late Night" alone.
