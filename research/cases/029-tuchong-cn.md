## What Works (in our context)

Facts: place-tab + chronological feed is a common Chinese photography community model; worth seeing as a counter-example to our personal-archive design.

## What Does Not Work

Facts: platform, not personal archive; commercial weight is high; identity = user-handle, not personal voice.

## Potential Lesson for Our Project (interpretation)

Useful as a NON-WESTERN reference (the brief explicitly called for Chinese/Korean/Asian cases). Confirms that Chinese photo communities tend to be platform-based, not personal-archive-based. The personal-archive pattern we are researching is rarer in CN context, which itself is a useful finding.
