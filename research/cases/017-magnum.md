## What Works

Facts: photographer-as-primary-entity scales to ~100 with normal site architecture; story/chronicles provide chronological discovery.

## What Does Not Work

Facts: archive depth (millions of images) is gated behind per-photographer profiles - finding a specific photo requires either profile navigation or search.

## Potential Lesson for Our Project (interpretation)

Even a multi-decade archive stays usable if the primary entry is the photographer, not the photo. Confirms our hunch that a Gallery-first model can scale, but suggests ALSO providing a strong search/filter to bypass navigation.
