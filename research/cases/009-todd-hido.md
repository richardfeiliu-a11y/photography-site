## What Works

Interpretation: strong evidence for slideshow mode preserving photographic pacing (one image commands full attention).

## What Does Not Work

## Potential Lesson for Our Project (interpretation)

Viewer-pattern evidence: arrow-key slideshow suits moody sequential work; but for trip galleries users may need progress indication (photo N of M) which this site lacks - worth testing in stage 2.
