## What Works (for our context)

Facts: 700+ exhibitions paginated with reverse-chronological order and explicit date ranges survives 50 years. Shows that "load more" pagination is viable at 5x our worst-case scale.

## What Does Not Work (for our context)

Facts: no place filter, no photographer filter, no subject filter at the list level.

## Potential Lesson for Our Project (interpretation)

Two borrowable ideas: (1) explicit date-range strings (e.g. "Mar 26, 2026 - Jun 21, 2026") communicate duration at a glance; (2) "Load More" + reverse chronological is a workable default when the archive exceeds a single page.
