## Potential Lesson for Our Project (interpretation)

(1) Year-range-in-title is a cheap scalable chronology metadata pattern. (2) A separate playground archive can coexist with a structured main site. (3) Desktop-only archives violate our mobile-first requirement - useful negative constraint.
