## What Works

Facts: search-first archive with honest scale numbers (1163 photos, 2003-present); landmark taxonomy inside one city; documentary projects preserved separately.

Interpretation: proves full-text search is a legitimate third axis alongside time and place once volume passes ~1000 images.

## What Does Not Work

Facts: commercial furniture (cart/reviews/newsletter) surrounds the archive heavily.

## Potential Lesson for Our Project (interpretation)

If our archive ever exceeds several hundred galleries, a search box may do more for old-work discoverability than any hierarchy - a hypothesis stage 2 should weigh against the desire for browsable structure.
