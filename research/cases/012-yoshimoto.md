## Place Handling

Place IS the project: every photograph is a SPECIFIC Tokyo location revisited across decades. JINDAIJI demonstrates a single-place long-running focus.

---

## Time Handling

Time IS the project: explicit 1984-2026 comparison. Each comparison unit = two photos from the same place, different decades.

---

## Repeated Visit Problem

This case IS the repeated-visit case study. Approach: TWO PHOTOS PER PLACE from different eras, side by side. Not "merge all visits" and not "split per visit" - it is "pair per place across years".

Structure:

    Place
    +-- 1984 photo
    +-- 2025 photo
    (the SAME place, same viewpoint, decades apart)
